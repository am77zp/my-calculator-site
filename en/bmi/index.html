BMI Calculator

Enter your height and weight to calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI).

This result is for reference only and is not a medical diagnosis.

How to use

  1. Enter your height in centimeters.
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see your BMI and category.

How it works

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ (height (m) × height (m)). This calculator uses the WHO international classification: under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–25 is normal, 25–30 is overweight, and 30 or above is obese.

Examples

Height 170 cm, weight 65 kg → BMI 22.5 (Normal)
Height 160 cm, weight 82 kg → BMI 32.0 (Obese)

FAQ

Is the BMI standard the same worldwide?

This calculator uses the WHO international standard. Some countries, including Korea, use slightly different cut-offs.

Is BMI accurate for very muscular people?

Not always — BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, so it can overestimate body fat in muscular individuals.
