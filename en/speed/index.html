Speed, Distance, Time Calculator

Choose what to solve for and enter the other two values to get the result instantly.

How to use

  1. Choose what to solve for (speed / distance / time).
  2. Enter the other two values (distance in km, time in hours (decimals allowed) or h:m, speed in km/h).
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the result instantly.

How it works

The three values follow speed = distance ÷ time (v = d/t). To find speed: distance ÷ time. To find distance: speed × time. To find time: distance ÷ speed.

Examples

Example 1 — In "find speed" mode, distance=150 km and time=2.5 hours (2h30m) gives a speed of 60 km/h.
Example 2 — In "find time" mode, distance=100 km and speed=40 km/h gives a time of 2.5 hours (2h30m).

FAQ

Can I enter time as hours and minutes instead of decimal hours?

Yes, both decimal hours and h:m entry are supported. For example, 2 hours 30 minutes equals 2.5 hours.

Can I use mph or other units instead of km/h?

This calculator doesn't convert units itself — it uses whatever distance/speed unit system you enter consistently. To work in mph, enter distance in miles and speed in mph.
