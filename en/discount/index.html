Discount Calculator

Enter the original price and discount rate to calculate the sale price.

How to use

  1. Enter the original price before the discount.
  2. Enter the discount rate as a percentage.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the sale price and savings.

How it works

Sale price = original price × (1 − discount rate ÷ 100). The amount you save is the original price minus the sale price.

Examples

Original price 50,000, discount 20% → Sale price 40,000, you save 10,000.
Original price 129,000, discount 30% → Sale price 90,300, you save 38,700.

FAQ

Can I calculate the discount rate from two prices instead?

This calculator goes from price + rate to the sale price; a reverse calculator may be added later.

Does this include tax?

No — it applies the discount to whatever price you enter, with no tax added or removed.
