Unit Converter

Convert between length, weight, and volume units.

How to use

  1. Choose a category: length, weight, or volume.
  2. Enter the value and choose the units to convert from and to.
  3. Click "Convert" to see the result.

How it works

Every unit is first converted to a common base unit (meter for length, kilogram for weight, liter for volume), then converted into the target unit.

Examples

100 cm → = 1 m
10 lb → ≈ 4.53592 kg

FAQ

Which units are supported?

Length: mm, cm, m, km, in, ft, mi. Weight: mg, g, kg, t, lb, oz. Volume: ml, l, gal, cup.

How precise are the results?

Results are shown to up to 6 decimal places.
